Why the dialog said “Unable to load opted counts” — and how to get the numbers yourself

The analytics service is up and healthy. GET /api/health reports sourceDatabase: connected, and every data route under /api/analytics/* answers normally. It is the dialog's specific endpoint that does not exist on the server, so the UI renders its fallback error. Nothing is down on your side.

Working endpoints you can open directly in a browser (no login needed):

  • …execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/api/analytics/submissions → 381 submissions, 182 team submissions, 311 remaining, 36.9%
  • /api/analytics/dashboard → full overview (registrations, teams, trends, geography, themes, demographics)
  • /api/analytics/themes → registrations per sub-theme (the table above)
  • /api/analytics/districts → district tracker; supports ?state=Maharashtra, ?category=General Category, ?sector=Women %26 Child
  • /api/analytics/registrations, /api/analytics/teams, /api/analytics/geography, /api/analytics/colleges

For your dev team, the two fix candidates are: (a) point the dialog at an existing route such as /api/analytics/themes or /api/analytics/districts, or (b) add the missing sub-theme-counts route on the analytics backend. Also worth raising: the analytics API currently answers without any authentication or key — it is fully reachable by anyone who knows the URL, so it should be locked down behind the app.

Re-run python3 fetch.py && python3 aggregate.py && python3 make_report.py in this folder to refresh every number.
